Design

DESIGN can be structured, scientific, and expressively creative.

To me, 'DESIGN' connects:

  • planningandfreedom
  • structureandflow
  • mindandbody
  • processandexpression
  • logicandcreativeness
  • managementandplayfulness

DESIGN is playtime that brings a vibrant spirit to everything. We can DESIGN our plans and we can get creative, we can build a system or a process and decide to completely let go, have fun and ultimately be free.

Finding an unforced, egoless flow is what I seek in my personal life and what I constantly strive to DESIGN for clients, customers, end-users, and recipients of my work.
